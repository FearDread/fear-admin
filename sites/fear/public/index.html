About

        
Systems administrator at heart. Developer by necessity.

        
I’m Garrett, a Linux systems administrator with a background in full-stack development and infrastructure engineering. I work with AWS, networking and DevOps tooling, and I run my own self-hosted web application stacks. Most of my work sits between an application and the machines beneath it, the place where small oversights tend to become large outages.

        
Outside of work I’m a committed technology enthusiast: games, computers and anything with a blinking light. I also travel, camp, hike and rock climb. I’m easy to work with and I communicate plainly, especially when something has gone wrong.

About

        
Sysadmin by trade. Developer by poor impulse control.

        
I’m Garrett: a Linux systems administrator who wandered into full-stack development and never found the exit. I live in the gap between “works on my machine” and “why is production on fire,” armed with AWS, networking, and DevOps for both starting and extinguishing it. I self-host my own stacks because I enjoy being the single point of failure.

        
Off the clock I’m a big-time nerd: games, computers, anything with a blinking light. I also camp, hike, and rock climb, hobbies where gravity is the SLA and the postmortem is mercifully short. Easy to get along with, good at explaining outages in plain English, then again, more gently.
